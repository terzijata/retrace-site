Retrace — Terms of Sale

Last updated: 2026-09-20

These terms cover the purchase of the optional Backup & restore unlock for Retrace. The Retrace extension itself is free to install and use; buying the unlock is entirely optional. By purchasing, you agree to these terms.

What you're buying

A one-time license to use the Backup & restore feature inside Retrace — the ability to export everything Retrace has stored (your sessions, Spaces, activity log, and settings) to a file, and to import that file to merge or fully restore on another device or after a reinstall. It is a single, one-time payment of $10 — not a subscription. There are no recurring charges.

Your license

Your purchase grants you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Backup & restore feature. You may use your key on the devices where you personally use Retrace. Please don't share, resell, or publish your license key — it's for your own use.

Payment and merchant of record

Payments are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd), acting as the merchant of record. This means Paddle handles the transaction, receipts, and any applicable sales tax or VAT on our behalf. Your purchase is also subject to Paddle's own buyer terms. The Retrace extension is not involved in the payment and never sees your payment details.

Delivery

After a successful purchase you'll receive a license key by email, in the form RTRC-…. Keys are issued manually, so delivery is usually within a few hours and no later than 2 business days. Paste your key into Retrace › Settings › Backup & restore › Unlock to activate the feature. If your key hasn't arrived, contact us (below) and we'll sort it out.

Offline activation

Your license key is verified entirely on your device, using a cryptographic signature check against a public key built into the extension. No server is ever contacted to activate or validate it — this is what keeps Retrace's "no network requests" promise true even for paid users. It also means we cannot remotely disable a key once it has been issued.
