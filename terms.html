Last updated: 2026-09-20

What you're buying

A one-time license for Retrace Pro, which unlocks a bundle of features inside Retrace: Backup & restore (export/import your data, with optional password encryption and an optional connected backup file that auto-mirrors to a folder you choose), custom keyboard shortcuts and a command palette, auto-archive rules, and reusable templates. It is a single, one-time payment of $10 — not a subscription. There are no recurring charges, and future additions to the Retrace Pro bundle are included.

Delivery

After a successful purchase you'll receive a license key by email, in the form RTRC-…. Keys are issued manually, so delivery is usually within a few hours and no later than 2 business days. Paste your key into Retrace › Settings › Backup & restore › Unlock to activate the feature. If your key hasn't arrived, contact us (below) and we'll sort it out.

Offline activation

Your license key is verified entirely on your device, using a cryptographic signature check against a public key built into the extension. No server is ever contacted to activate or validate it — this is what keeps Retrace's "no network requests" promise true even for paid users. It also means we cannot remotely disable a key once it has been issued.
