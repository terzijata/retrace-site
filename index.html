Everything you need to retrace your steps

Every open window at a glance

Each window gets a name and a one-line summary, with its tab groups and what's playing. Press 1–9 to jump there, Alt Shift W for the next one. Group, tidy or sleep tabs in a click.

Close a window, lose nothing

Busy windows are kept by themselves and follow along as you work. Close one by accident? It's still there for two weeks — keep it for good in one click.

Sessions that describe themselves

A search shows as what you searched for, a video by its title, a repo by its name — never just “google.com”.

Spaces that fill themselves

Start with Work, Personal, Learning, Shopping and Travel — or make your own, with sub-spaces inside. Retrace learns what each is about and files new sessions for you.

Go back to any moment

Every few minutes Retrace notes what's open. After a crash — or for that tab you closed an hour ago — slide back through the day.

All your music in one list

One entry per song, whether you heard it on YouTube, Spotify or SoundCloud — playlists, and your history brought in from Spotify or YouTube. Pro adds the songs you play by themselves, and a monthly recap.
