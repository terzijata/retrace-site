Get your tabs back after the browser closes or crashes

For Chrome and Edge.

Right after closing something by mistake

  • Press Ctrl+Shift+T (⌘+Shift+T on a Mac) to reopen the last closed tab or window. Press it again to go further back.
  • Or open the menu › History › Recently closed.

After a crash or restart

  • If the browser offers Restore when it opens, click it straight away.
  • To always reopen where you left off: Settings › On start-up › Continue where you left off.
  • Otherwise, search your History (Ctrl+H) for pages you remember.

Never lose them again

Retrace keeps a private recovery timeline: every few minutes it notes which pages are open in each window. After a crash — or when you need the tab you closed an hour ago — open Retrace's Settings, slide back through the day, or search for the page, and reopen it.

  • Save important windows as sessions and pin them: pinned sessions reopen when the browser starts.
  • Everything stays on your device — the timeline is never uploaded.
