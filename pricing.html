What exactly you're buying

Product: Retrace Pro — a one-time digital license that unlocks the Retrace Pro features inside the Retrace browser extension, listed below.

Price: $10.00, charged once. It is not a subscription and there are no recurring fees.

What it does: Retrace Pro adds several features, all on your own device:

  • Backup & restore — export everything Retrace has stored to a file and merge it into Retrace on another device (the newer copy of anything wins; nothing is wiped); the export can be password-encrypted.
  • Connected backup file — point Retrace at a file once and it keeps it in two-way sync, encrypted by default; put it in a synced folder (Dropbox/Drive/OneDrive) for cross-device continuity, with no Retrace servers.
  • Custom keyboard shortcuts and a ⌘/Ctrl-K command palette to drive Retrace from the keyboard.
  • Tidy up your library — merge overlapping sessions and clear duplicates and old ones, with Undo.
  • Keep the recovery timeline for up to 30 days.
  • Auto-archive old sessions automatically, and save reusable templates of tabs.

Delivery: after purchase you receive a license key by email, in the form RTRC-…. You paste it into Retrace › Settings › Backup & restore › Unlock to activate Retrace Pro. There is nothing to ship.

Platform: Retrace Pro is available in the Microsoft Edge version of Retrace. The Google Chrome version is free and does not include the paid features.
