Retrace — Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

Retrace itself is free to install. The only paid item is the optional Backup & restore unlock — a one-time $10 purchase that delivers a license key by email. This policy explains how refunds work for that purchase.

14-day, no-questions-asked refund

If you're not happy with the Backup & restore unlock for any reason, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase. You don't need to give a reason.

How to request a refund

Email retrace.contact@protonmail.com from (or mentioning) the email address you purchased with, and include your order number if you have it. We'll confirm and arrange the refund.

Because payments are handled by our payment provider, Paddle, acting as the merchant of record, you can also request a refund or find your order directly through Paddle's buyer support at paddle.net. Refunds are returned to your original payment method; the time to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

After a refund

Your Retrace license key is verified entirely offline on your device — there is no server, so we have no way to remotely disable a key that has already been issued. We therefore ask, in good faith, that you stop using the Backup & restore feature once a purchase has been refunded. Everything else in Retrace stays free to use as normal.

Questions

Anything about a purchase, a key, or a refund: retrace.contact@protonmail.com.
